# The Mini Sting

**Market Square, Caistor**

**9am Start**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## **Join the 2023 Caistor ‘Mini Sting’ Fun Run! **

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### The Caistor Mini Sting Fun Run is being held for the fifth time, alongside the ‘Sting 10k’ on Sunday 2nd July 2023.

The course takes in 2 laps of the streets of Caistor on a fully Marshalled route, starting and finishing in the Market Square, where a carnival atmosphere is guaranteed. As a special feature for this year’s race, we have again included a mad 100m of colourful watery fun which should be welcome on a sunny July day!

The run starts at 9am and is open to all ages. Children under the age of 8 must be accompanied by an adult who will run with them around the course. 

**Adults can accompany up to three children but ALL adults do need to pay their own entry fee to take part.**

Entry is just £3 per runner and all children completing the run will receive a goody bag and a fabulous bespoke  Mini Sting medal. 

There will also be prizes awarded on the day for such things as best fancy dress and although the main aim of this event is a 'Fun' run there will also be prizes awarded for the1st placed boy & girl for those that wish to be a little bit more competitive.

**We don’t think you can have this much fun anywhere on a Sunday morning for just £3!**
