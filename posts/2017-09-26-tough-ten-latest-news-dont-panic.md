# Tough Ten - Latest News - Don't Panic!

Unfortunately, we have received the news today that "The Swallow Inn" is closing with immediate effect. Just to clarify, the race will go ahead as planned and we still have access to the car park. Alternative plans have been made to ensure everyone still gets their hot dog and we will have some hot dogs to sell to family and friends. We will also provide tea and coffee.
