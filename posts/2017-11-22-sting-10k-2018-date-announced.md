# Sting 10K 2018 Date Announced

![](2017-11-22-sting-10k-2018-date-announced/19748736_10154773355763450_8023536533277725195_n.jpg)

![Julie and Debby from Scunthorpe & District a leaping - Sting 2017](2017-11-22-sting-10k-2018-date-announced/19748736_10154773355763450_8023536533277725195_n-339x340.jpg)

*Julie and Debby from Scunthorpe & District a leaping - Sting 2017*

We are pleased to announce that the date for the Sting in the Tail 10K 2018 will be **Sunday 8th July 2018**.

We will be opening entries for the Sting 2018 a little earlier than usual. Details of when this takes place will be announced soon, both here and on our [Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/groups/caistorrunningclub/).
