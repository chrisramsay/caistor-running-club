# Entries Now Open for the Sting 10k 2018

![](2018-01-09-entries-now-open-for-the-sting-10k-2018/26169044_1977356855624502_5031391085139399929_n.jpg)

![Sting 10k](2018-01-09-entries-now-open-for-the-sting-10k-2018/26169044_1977356855624502_5031391085139399929_n-720x292.jpg)

Entries are now open for the 2018 Brianplant Sting in the Tail 10k and based on last years sell out event it would be a wise move to secure your place as early as possible.

We are expecting another sell out year, especially with the additional incentive of a beer and Lincolnshire sausage for every finisher (that's as well as the now iconic Sting vest!).

Entries for the race are via Sports Entry Solutions and can be found [here](https://www.sportsentrysolutions.com/new_race_page.php?recordID=200706).

We look forward to seeing you all on the day for what promises to be another great event.
