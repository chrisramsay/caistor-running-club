# Re-launch of CRC Newsletter!!

![](2019-02-28-re-launch-of-crc-newsletter/skynews-thepapers-v2-2-1_3577490-1.jpg)

![](2019-02-28-re-launch-of-crc-newsletter/skynews-thepapers-v2-2-1_3577490-1024x576.jpg)

I’m planning to re-launch the club newsletter “**The Caistor Running Times**”, possibly on a quarterly basis. For those new to the club, the past editions can be downloaded on this website in the Archives section. It was always a good read, but took a break from production after the September 2016 edition.

In order for it to be a success I would need to have lots of contributions from members. If anyone would like to suggest anything, whether it be a race report, an interesting article you have read, a review or a suggestion for a regular feature please let me know. I'm also looking for your photos of "CRC Members On Tour". We are proud to wear our club colours and often photograph ourselves away from the local patch, whether it be parkrun tourism or holidays abroad. I'd like to include a few of these!

Send an email to <CRCrunningtimes@gmail.com>.  I will be asking one or two people specifically for their input but if anyone would like to send something in, please do so. Recipes, if not family secrets, are always welcome too!!

I look forward to receiving your contributions! Please help me make this a success!

Thank you

Emma Morrison
