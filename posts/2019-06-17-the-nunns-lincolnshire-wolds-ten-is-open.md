# The Nunns Lincolnshire Wolds Ten is Open!

![](2019-06-17-the-nunns-lincolnshire-wolds-ten-is-open/42359186_2287160931313425_1986908693546926080_o.jpg)

![Helen & Andy on the W10 2018](2019-06-17-the-nunns-lincolnshire-wolds-ten-is-open/42359186_2287160931313425_1986908693546926080_o-1024x683.jpg)

*Helen & Andy on the W10 2018*

The Nunns Lincolnshire Wolds Ten, taking place over 10 miles of beautiful countryside in the Lincolnshire wolds, is in its 37^(th) year and [Caistor Running Club](http://46.51.166.150/), known for the ever-popular Sting in the Tail 10K, are proud to host.

The race takes place on **Sunday 22nd September 2019** and starts at 10am.

Entry is just £15 affiliated (£17 unaffiliated) and includes a race memento to take away with you.
