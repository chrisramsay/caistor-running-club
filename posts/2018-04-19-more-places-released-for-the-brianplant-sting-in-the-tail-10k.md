# More places released for the Brianplant Sting in the Tail 10k!

![](2018-04-19-more-places-released-for-the-brianplant-sting-in-the-tail-10k/26169044_1977356855624502_5031391085139399929_n.jpg)

Due to the hard work of our technical team in ensuring Health and Safety requirements can be met we are delighted to say we can now release an additional 130 places for this year's race!

So if you missed out on a place when the initial entries went live, this is your chance to secure a place in one of the friendliest 10k races around!

The additional entries will be available via the [Sports Entry Solutions portal](https://www.sportsentrysolutions.com/new_race_page.php?recordID=200706) from **9 am on Friday 20^(th) April. **

It is unlikely that any further places will be available once these are taken, so if you want to run this fantastic race then make sure you snap one up!
