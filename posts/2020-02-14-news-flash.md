# ** News Flash!**

THERE WILL BE NO CLUB RUNS THIS WEEKEND

For safety reasons the club runs this weekend, February 15th and 16th have been postponed to a later date. Storm Dennis is due to hit the country.

The weather warning has now been raised to Amber for both wind and rain. The potential risk of falling branches and/or trees on both the Saturday and Sunday routes means that we cannot guarantee the safety of our runners. Stay safe folks.
