# Lincolnshire Wolds Tough Ten is back for 2023!

![](2023-05-04-lincolnshire-wolds-tough-ten-is-back-for-2023/42417310_1707844385993216_957819479859920896_n-510x340-1.jpg)

Exciting news! The classic Lincolnshire Wolds Tough Ten is back in its new, low-key time trial format for 2023. 

The event will be taking place on Wednesday 21st June 2023 at 7:00pm, and costs just £5 to enter.   
For more information and to enter, please [click here](https://www.theentrypoint.co.uk/events/lincolnshire-wolds-tough-ten-time-trial-2023-bGXzW4x2EOKjm83)
