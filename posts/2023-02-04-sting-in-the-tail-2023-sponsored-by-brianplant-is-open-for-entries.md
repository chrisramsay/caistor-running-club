# Sting in the Tail 2023 - Sponsored by Brianplant is Open for Entries

![](2023-02-04-sting-in-the-tail-2023-sponsored-by-brianplant-is-open-for-entries/293795857_10224897384655277_2314123706701780358_n.jpg)

Everyone’s favourite 10K the Caistor Sting 10K is back this year on Sunday 2nd July and entries are now open! Unlike most other things these days (including running races) it’s actually even cheaper than last year: just £17 (£15 UKA affiliated) including a beer, an ice cream and a unique memento (not a vest :)) for all finishers!

Enter here: [Sting 2023 Entry Link](https://theentrypoint.co.uk/events/caistor-sting-10k-2023-sponsored-by-brianplant-ro5p0O6NWOnEV8w)

Go on, get entered now before it sells out!
