# Lincolnshire Wolds Tough Ten is back for 2022!

Exciting news! The classic Lincolnshire Wolds Tough Ten is back in a new, low-key time trial format for 2022. 

The event will be taking place on Wednesday 22nd June at 7:00pm, and costs just £5 to enter.   
For more information and to enter, please [click here](https://theentrypoint.co.uk/events/lincolnshire-wolds-tough-ten-time-trial-bGXzW4x2EOKjm83)
