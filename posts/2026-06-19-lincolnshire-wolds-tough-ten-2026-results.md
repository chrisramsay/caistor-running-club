# Lincolnshire Wolds Tough Ten 2026 - Results

Huge thanks to all the volunteers who once again made this year's Tough Ten possible!  
  
Many thanks also to all the runners who took the time on the night and afterwards to say how much they'd enjoyed the event - it's thanks to help and support of the volunteers and the positive feedback from runners that the Wolds Tough Ten keeps going!  
  
Congratulations to all this year's participants, including returning 1st finisher Martin Troop and Charlie Thornton who broke her own Female Course record!
