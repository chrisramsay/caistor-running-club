# Entries Open for The Sting 2019

![](2018-11-16-entries-open-for-the-sting-2019/36850508_10156730006295312_963934302108647424_o.jpg)

![](2018-11-16-entries-open-for-the-sting-2019/sting_2018_header.jpg)

Entries are now open for the 2019 Brianplant Sting in the Tail 10k and based on last years sell out event it would be a wise move to secure your place as early as possible.

*New this year - Chip Timing*

Again, we are expecting sell out year, especially with the additional incentive of a beer and Lincolnshire sausage for every finisher (that's as well as the now iconic Sting vest!).

Money from this race will as usual go to several local good causes.

We look forward to seeing you all on the day for what promises to be another great event.
