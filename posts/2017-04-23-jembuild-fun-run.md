# JemBuild Fun Run

#### STOP PRESS - ENTRIES WILL BE AVAILABLE ON THE DAY FROM THE CAISTOR RUNNING CLUB GAZEBO IN THE MARKETPLACE

This year, for the very first time, Caistor Running Club are pleased to announce the JemBuild Fun Run.

This is an event open to all ages and designed for those that are not old enough to enter the classic 10km ‘Sting’ or haven’t quite set their sights on such a challenge.

The event will take place at 09:00 an hour before the start of the 10km road race and will be approximately 2km.  
It will be run on the same weekend as the annual street party – “Caistor goes … Mardi Gras!” and competitors are encouraged to dress up. Spot prizes will be given out for those runners who embrace the ‘fun’ of the race.

The race costs £5 to enter with 200 places available on-line.

All runners need to collect their race numbers at Caistor Town Hall on the morning of the race between 8:00 - 8:45 am. There will be a short warm-up at 8:45 in the Market Square before the start of the race.

For additional information and to [enter the JemBuild Caistor Fun Run please go here](https://www.sportsentrysolutions.com/new_race_page.php?recordID=200526).
