# 10th Sting in the Tail 10k Open for Entries

![](2022-02-10-10th-sting-in-the-tail-10k-open-for-entries/36978822_2169103563119163_6107244878444363776_o.jpg)

Entries are open for the challenging, but very friendly "Sting in the Tail 10k". Once again generously sponsored by Brianplant and a whole host of other businesses, this event promises to be more exciting than before, with the iconic yellow vest earning "King of the Sting" challenge returning for the 2022 race.

Enter here: <https://www.theentrypoint.co.uk/events/caistor-sting-10k-sponsored-by-brianplant-AoNja4X1m906Vbn>

As usual, the iconic Sting vest, a beer and a sausage will be on offer for successful finishers. The race will also be chip timed, as on previous occasions.

Go on, get entered now before it sells out!
